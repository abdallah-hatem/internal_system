# B2 — Recording deliveries and losses

Branch `feature/arrivals-b2`, code commit `f21c312` (based on `93b2ac9`).

## What was built

- **Endpoints**, all `RolesGuard` + `@Roles('CORE_PARTNER')`:
  `POST /shipping/legs/:id/deliveries`, `DELETE /shipping/deliveries/:id`,
  `POST /shipping/legs/:id/losses`, `DELETE /shipping/losses/:id` (take a loss back).
  The logic is in a new `DeliveriesService` (`modules/shipping/deliveries.service.ts`),
  exported from the shipping module.
- **One loader, one writer** (`common/shipment-load.ts`): `loadShipment` (the rows
  `shipmentPosition` reads; `GET /cycles/:id/shipment` now uses it too), `lockCycle`,
  `syncLegStatuses` (every writer calls it, inside its transaction, to store each leg's
  `status` and `arrivedOn` as the position reads them), and `legsThatMustHaveDelivered`
  (which legs a cycle status needs a delivery on — read by the transition gate and by
  deleting a delivery).
- **Concurrency.** Every write takes `SELECT id FROM import_cycles WHERE id = $1 FOR UPDATE`
  in its transaction and only then reads the position it checks. The cycle row, not
  the leg: leg 2 can only deliver what leg 1 delivered, so writes on two legs race too.
  Tested twice: a unit fake that models the lock, and `deliveries.db.spec.ts` against a
  throwaway Postgres (opt-in, `INTEGRATION_DATABASE_URL`).
- **Losses** are `ShipmentLoss` rows valued at the costing service's landed unit cost
  (read in the transaction: `computeCycleLandedCosts` gained an optional `db`), cost
  rounded half up to 2 places. No ledger row. `common/cycle-losses.ts` exports
  `cycleLossesEgp(db, cycleId)` for B3 to wire into settlement and analytics.
- **The old leg form.** `PUT /shipping/legs/:id` with `arrivedOn` and
  `POST /cycles/:id/shipping-legs` with `arrivedOn` record one delivery of what is still on
  the way; nothing left → nothing written. A null `arrivedOn` and any `status` are ignored.
  Moving `departedOn` after a delivery it would postdate → `DELIVERY_BEFORE_DEPARTURE`.
  Changing a leg's cost (basis or EGP total) once any unit was received or lost →
  `LEG_COST_LOCKED`.
- **Cycle gates** (`assertShippingReached`): ARRIVED_UAE needs a delivery on leg 1 (China),
  ARRIVED_EGYPT one on the last leg; departures as before.
- **Frozen cycle**: SETTLEMENT / CLOSED → `CYCLE_SETTLING`; CANCELLED → `CYCLE_CLOSED`, for
  deliveries, losses, take-backs and deleting a delivery.
- **check-data**: 12.3 replaced by 12.3 "a leg delivered or lost more than entered it",
  12.4 "received + lost > ordered", 12.5 "loss cost ≠ qty × unit cost", 12.6 "a batch on a
  cycle with legs that came from no delivery".
- **Codes** added to `en.json` and `ar.json`: `CYCLE_CLOSED`, `CYCLE_SETTLING`,
  `DELIVERY_BEFORE_DEPARTURE`, `DELIVERY_EXCEEDS_IN_TRANSIT`, `DELIVERY_IN_USE`,
  `LEG_COST_LOCKED`, `LOSS_EXCEEDS_IN_TRANSIT`; entities `shipmentDelivery`, `shipmentLoss`.
  `LEG_NOT_ARRIVED` now says "record a delivery".
- **Found on the way and fixed:** editing a leg without sending `currency` reset a USD leg
  to EGP (the merged cost fields left the currency out).

## Guesses (open until confirmed)

1. **A loss does not need the leg to have departed.** The spec names no such refusal;
   goods can be lost in a warehouse. A leg with nothing entered has nothing to lose anyway.
2. **On the last leg a loss may take what was delivered but not received** (the "waiting"
   units), as §1 says. On earlier legs only what is on the way.
3. **The old date checks keep their codes.** A PUT/POST with `arrivedOn` and no departure
   is still `ARRIVAL_WITHOUT_DEPARTURE`, before departure `ARRIVAL_BEFORE_DEPARTURE`
   (TC-LEG-04 pins them). The new endpoint uses `LEG_NOT_DEPARTED` /
   `DELIVERY_BEFORE_DEPARTURE`.
4. **A PUT arrival with nothing on the way is a no-op even with a different date.** The
   stored arrival does not move; correcting a date is delete-and-record.
5. **"A leg's cost" = its basis and EGP total.** Re-sending the same figures, or changing
   rate and pieces to the same total, is allowed after the lock. Adding a new leg after a
   receipt is not locked (only possible on a China cycle missing leg 2).
6. **The gate checks the last *recorded* leg** for ARRIVED_EGYPT, so a China cycle whose
   second leg was never added needs its one leg to have delivered.
7. **The gate applies to every target status**, not only the two arrivals: moving to
   VERIFICATION or SELLING re-checks the deliveries the status implies.
8. **Deliveries are allowed in any open cycle status**, including PLANNING. Only the frozen
   statuses refuse.
9. **Notifications** fire when a leg becomes ARRIVED (all accounted), not on each delivery.
10. `transition` does not take the cycle lock, so deleting a delivery at the very moment a
    cycle moves to an arrival could slip past the `DELIVERY_IN_USE` status check.
11. Deleting a delivery is refused as `DELIVERY_IN_USE` with `params.why` =
    `received` | `carried` | `status`; one translated sentence covers all three.
