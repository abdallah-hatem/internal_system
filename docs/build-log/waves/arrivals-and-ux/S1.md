# S1 — the demo seed's paid orders with no payments

Branch `fix/demo-seed-payments`, from `b571e63`.

## Root cause

`apps/api/prisma/seed.ts` (the demo level) wrote both sale orders straight to
their final state: SO-DEMO-0001 as `PARTIALLY_PAID` with outstanding 2,200 of
7,200, SO-DEMO-0002 as `PAID`. No `Payment`, no `PaymentAllocation`, no
`PAYMENT_RECEIVED` ledger row. So the orders said 45,000 had come in, the
Payments list was empty and the dashboard's Collected (a sum over `payments`)
read 0.00.

It also wrote a `SALE_REVENUE` ledger INFLOW per order, which the app stopped
doing on 2026-08-22 (§9: the ledger records money as it is received; confirming
raises no entry).

## Fix

- Orders are created `CONFIRMED`, owing their whole total — the state the app
  leaves them in after Confirm.
- The money goes in through the app's own `PaymentsService` (`create` then
  `allocateToOrder`, as the customer page does), instantiated in the seed with
  the seed's Prisma client and a real `AuditService`. Chosen over copying the
  writes so there is one definition of what receiving money produces (rule 11),
  and so the seed runs the same guards: verified customer, not more than owed,
  not dated forward. It writes the payment, its `PAYMENT_RECEIVED` INFLOW, the
  allocation, the order's outstanding/status, and the audit rows.
  - Order 1: 5,000 cash of 7,200 → `PARTIALLY_PAID`, 2,200 owed (unchanged).
  - Order 2: 40,000 bank transfer → `PAID`.
- The `SALE_REVENUE` ledger rows are gone.
- Order numbers use the sales service's format, `ORD-YYYY-NNNNN`, built with the
  same `nextReferenceNumber`/`pad` helpers, so the first order made by hand after
  a demo reset continues at 00003. The `SO-DEMO-` style was not deliberate —
  nothing referenced it. Cycle codes stay `CYC-DEMO-00x`: e2e
  (`16-settlements`) selects by them, and the review raised order numbers only.

## check-data

New check 13.1, *order shows money paid that no payment allocated to it*: for
every order past draft and not cancelled, `total − outstanding` must equal the
allocations of its non-reversed payments. Orders with a credit-note return are
skipped — the credit lowers outstanding with no allocation and is clamped at
zero, so the paid figure cannot be rebuilt from the tables.

## Evidence (throwaway databases in `motorcycle_parts_db`, then dropped)

- New seed, `s1_check`: migrate deploy + `prisma db seed` → all 22 checks 0,
  exit 0. Orders `ORD-2026-00001 PARTIALLY_PAID 7200/2200 allocated 5000`,
  `ORD-2026-00002 PAID 40000/0 allocated 40000`; two payments, two
  `PAYMENT_RECEIVED` rows, four audit rows. Seed run a second time: skipped,
  still 2 payments.
- Old seed (from `b571e63`), `s1_old`: check 13.1 = **2**, exit 1. Check 13
  (payment attached to no order) stayed 0 — it could never see this.

No jest test: the change is the seed's sequence of service calls against a
database; the two check-data runs are the proof. The shared database was not
touched.
