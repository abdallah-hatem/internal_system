# W2 — Walk-in on the sale form

Branch `feature/w2-walk-in-form`, based on `871c8dd`.

## What was built

- **`SaleOrderForm`**: a **Walk-in** switch (`role="switch"`, `type="button"`) on the Customer
  label row. On: the customer picker and its `+` are not rendered; a payment-method picker takes
  their place (`PAYMENT_METHODS` imported from `RecordPaymentDialog`, labels from
  `codeLabel('paymentMethod', …)`, CASH first); the channel is set to B2C through the existing
  `applyChannel`, so untouched lines take the retail list price and a typed price stays
  (`priceTyped`); the channel picker is disabled; *Save as draft* and *Create & confirm* give way
  to one **Sell & take payment**; the total shows a "Paid" badge and "Paid in full now · <method>".
  Off: the channel it found is put back (the customer was only hidden, never cleared), untouched
  lines re-price, and both buttons return.
- **Payload**: `{ walkIn: true, channel: 'B2C', currency, confirm: true, payment: { method }, items }`
  — no `customerId`. The same idempotency key and the synchronous `submitting` ref guard as U1.
  Refusals go through `useApiError()` into the form's alert; the form keeps every value.
- **Sales page**: list rows, phone cards, the order detail and the cancel dialog say "Walk-in"
  (translated) instead of a name, with no link (the record has no page), and the outstanding cell
  reads "— (paid on the spot)". Search matches the translated word. A walk-in's return offers a
  money refund only (the server refuses credit, `WALK_IN_REFUND_IN_MONEY`). A walk-in sale's toast
  is "Sold and paid", and it also refreshes payments and analytics.
- **Keys** under `sales` in both `en.json` and `ar.json`: `walkIn`, `walkInCustomer`,
  `paymentMethod`, `sellAndTakePayment`, `walkInPaidBy`, `walkInSold`, `walkInNoBalance`.
- The existing form tests used a customer *named* "Walk-in"; renamed to a B2C shop "Mona Retail",
  since the walk-in record never reaches a picker (W1 filters it).

## Guesses

- The switch keeps the hidden customer and puts it back when turned off, rather than clearing it.
- A walk-in return offers only a cash refund (the server already refuses credit).
- The channel picker is shown disabled at B2C rather than hidden, so the seller sees why the
  prices are retail.
